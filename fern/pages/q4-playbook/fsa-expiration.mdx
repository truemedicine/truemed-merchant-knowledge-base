After Cyber Monday your promo calendar thins out, and once the gift-shipping cutoff passes, most brands go quiet for the rest of the year. This is the window where HSA/FSA earns its keep.

Most FSA funds expire December 31. In 2023, 47% of account holders forfeited money, an average of $422 each. That is a real deadline with real money behind it, which means you do not need to discount to create urgency. Your customers already have it. FSA spending is also self-purchase rather than gift-buying, so it keeps converting in the weeks when holiday gifting has stopped.

**What you're launching:** two emails, three ads, one feed post, one story slide, and one copy swap on your abandoned-cart flow. Every word is written below. Plan on an afternoon.

<Note>
**The message:** Most FSA funds expire December 31. Spend them here before they're gone.

Say "save an average of 30% for qualified customers" and "most FSA funds expire December 31." Never say "covered," "free," or "everything qualifies," and never tell an individual customer their funds will expire: some plans carry grace periods or partial rollovers. Every template below is pre-checked.
</Note>

## Your ads

Live by December 15.

Ad costs fall off a cliff after Cyber Monday, which makes December the cheapest reach you will buy all quarter. It is also the one month of the year when HSA/FSA holders are actively hunting for somewhere to spend before their balance disappears. You are not persuading anyone to want something new here. You are telling them where to put money they are about to lose.

All three say the same thing: the deadline is coming, spend your funds here. They differ only in how hard they press. Run all three, then scale whichever framing your audience responds to.

**Ad 1: The plain deadline**

- Headline: Most FSA Funds Expire December 31
- Primary text: If you're eligible, use yours on [Brand] before the deadline and save an average of 30%* with pre-tax dollars.
- Overlay text: Most FSA funds expire Dec 31
- Disclaimer (on the creative): Truemed is for qualified customers. HSA/FSA tax savings vary. Learn more at truemed.com/disclosures.
- CTA: Shop Now

## Before you send

Two rules for this moment. Say "save an average of 30% for qualified customers," never "covered" or "everything qualifies." And say "most FSA funds expire December 31" rather than telling any individual customer that theirs will, since some plans carry grace periods or partial rollovers.

Writing your own variations? Always use our standard disclosure language: *Truemed is for qualified customers. HSA/FSA tax savings vary.
