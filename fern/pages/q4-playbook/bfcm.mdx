Your sale is planned and your sends are drafted. This page is the layer that makes them hit harder: eligible shoppers pay with pre-tax dollars, an average of ~30% in savings* on top of your sale price. That stack is the best effective price your customers will see all year, and most of them don't know they can use it.

**What you're launching:** two emails, three ads, one feed post, one story slide, and two settings you add once. The copy is written for you below.

<Note>
**The message:** Your discount + their pre-tax savings = the best price of the year.

Say "save an average of 30% for qualified customers." Never say "covered," "free," or "everything qualifies." Every template below is pre-checked.
</Note>

## Season-long settings

Live by November 20. Two places to surface HSA/FSA outside your campaign sends.

**Email footer line.** Add this once and leave it. It works year-round, on every send.

> We accept HSA/FSA. Qualified customers save an average of 30%* on [Brand]. [Learn more →]

**Abandoned-cart overlay.** Add this above your existing cart reminder copy. Unlike the footer, this copy changes with each moment: the FSA Expiration and New Year pages give you the updated version when their windows open.

> Still deciding? If you're HSA/FSA eligible, your cart is an average of 30%* less than it looks. Stack your pre-tax dollars with our BFCM sale before it ends.

## Your ads

Live by November 20. Paid runs ahead of the sale so shoppers learn they're eligible before they see your discount.

100M+ Americans hold an HSA or FSA, and BFCM week is when they're actively hunting for maximum value. Lead your paid creative with the stack; it's an angle no non-eligible competitor can copy.

**Ad 1 — The stack**

- Headline: Stack Your Black Friday Savings
- Primary text: [Insert Your Promo] on [Brand], plus an average of 30%* in pre-tax savings if you're HSA/FSA eligible. Two kinds of savings, one checkout.
- Overlay text: [Promo] + HSA/FSA = your best price of the year
- CTA: Shop Now

**Ad 2 — The reveal**

- Headline: We Accept HSA/FSA
- Primary text: Most people don't know they may be able to use pre-tax HSA/FSA dollars on [Brand]. This Black Friday, qualified customers stack an average of 30%* in savings on top of [Insert Your Promo].
- Overlay text: [Brand] accepts HSA/FSA
- CTA: Learn More

**Ad 3 — The deadline bridge**

- Headline: The Sale Ends. Your Funds Don't Have To
- Primary text: Use your HSA/FSA during [Brand]'s BFCM sale and get more from every pre-tax dollar. FSA funds expire Dec 31, so this is the season to spend them well.
- Overlay text: Sale price + pre-tax dollars, if eligible
- CTA: Shop the Sale

## Your emails and social

Black Friday through Cyber Monday.

<Tip>
Most merchants keep the subject line for the promo itself. That's fine: put HSA/FSA in the preview text, the line that shows next to your subject in the inbox. Both templates below include one.
</Tip>
